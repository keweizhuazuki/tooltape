    About this viewer & comparison

    This single HTML file works directly from disk with no dependencies, uploads, storage, or network requests. Open a ToolTape v1 or v2 file, filter calls by tool name or payload, and select any call to inspect its input and recorded result. Comparison checks call index, tool name, canonical input, status, and canonical output or error class, in order. Durations are displayed but do not count as divergence. A shorter or longer tape diverges at the first missing call. Files are limited to 16 MiB and 50,000 calls to keep local inspection responsive. JSON numbers are normalized without losing precision. Equality covers only captured fields; redacted or omitted fields cannot be compared. The v2 END footer confirms the recording session closed, not business success. A v2 recording without END is incomplete and cannot be used for safe replay. Legacy v1 completeness is unknown. Source and policy metadata are self-reported, and differing capture policies are flagged separately from event differences. Close or reload this tab to discard its data.
